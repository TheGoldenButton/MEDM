🎡 Emoji wheel

Press the index trigger on either controller (desktop: press L) to open the wheel — it opens in that hand and stays open; press again to close it and send. The golden ball rests in the centre. Point your other controller at it, hold its trigger (desktop: mouse button) and drag toward an emoji on the rim until the ball turns green, then let go to pick. Let go anywhere else and it returns to the centre. Closing the wheel sends your picks above your head for a few seconds; you can see it in the mirror.

🧚 Ask Spark

With the wheel closed, hold the index trigger on its own (desktop: hold L), speak your question, then let go. A quick tap still opens the wheel. A ball of light forms while you speak, then the fairy emerges and answers in emoji. It does not translate — it answers, using what you are looking at (in AR, the headset-camera view).

🤖 Spark agent mode

With the wheel closed, hold the trigger (desktop: L) and give Spark a command — "explore the room", "decorate it cozy", "go look left", "add some plants" — or hold without speaking and release for fully autonomous mode. Spark's ball takes over: it sees through its own camera (small preview panel at lower-left), avoids walls and objects, and builds 3D decorations (plants, lamps, rugs, disco balls, custom shapes…) and mood lighting that match the conversation vibe. To cancel: tap ✖ Cancel task, press B / Y or Esc. Spark may ask you a question before placing things — tap an option / type, or hold the trigger and answer by voice. It looks from several angles and re-checks each placement. Questions (what/why/how…) still get the normal emoji answer. Needs the Meta key for smart behaviour; without one an offline planner explores and decorates by keywords.

💙 Spark's child avatar

The short blue avatar (Spark) wanders around the room on its own. Every so often it notices you, turns its head, then strolls over slowly to say hi (sometimes with a wave), hangs around for a few seconds and drifts off again. Face it (it will come to you, or walk up to it), then send it some emoji — close your emoji wheel to send, or speak a voice translation. Spark receives your symbols plus what the avatar sees from its own eyes and replies as a young child in VR would. Watch: its own emoji wheel opens in front of it, its arm drags the golden ball to each symbol in turn — at a different speed every pick, sometimes eager, sometimes dawdling — its face becomes each emoji as it picks it (smile, frown, surprise, sleepy…), each pick plays that emoji's own single note on its wheel, pitched just like yours, with the same rising/falling shimmer when its wheel opens and closes, and the answer pops up above its head.

🎙️ Translate to emoji

Hold index + grip together on one controller with the wheel closed (desktop: hold V), or hold the trigger of the wheel hand while the wheel is open (desktop: hold L with the wheel open). Speak, then let go to send. Wave your free hand through the transcript box (desktop: Esc) to cancel.

📷 AR / VR

Tap AR Mode at the top (or the 📷 button in-headset) to switch between the virtual room and the real world through the headset cameras, without leaving the headset. Ask Spark uses that real-world view. Room fit: while in AR the headset scans your walls, ceiling height, tables, couches, beds, doors and windows. When you leave AR the virtual room resizes to your real space with stand-ins for that furniture, and in AR the Spark agent sees your real room (reconstructed top map + camera photo) and builds around it, keeping furniture and doors clear.

🕹️ Moving & pages

The left thumbstick (desktop: WASD, drag to look) walks you around, even with the wheel open. The right thumbstick left/right (desktop: Q/E or arrows) flips wheel pages, or snap-turns you when the wheel is closed. A (right controller) / Space (desktop) jumps. B / Y closes the wheel without sending. Hands only: beckon with a "come here" motion to walk forward; push an open palm away to walk backward; swipe your hand left or right through the air to turn; flick both palms up to jump. Pinches are for the wheel and Spark.

🔍 Search

With the wheel open, point your other controller at the search box under the page name and pull its trigger (desktop: click it and type, Enter to search). Results appear on wheel page 2 and clear when the wheel closes.

⚙️ Settings & hands

In VR, point at ⚙️ at the top of your view, pull the trigger, then tap ✏️ to type your API key with the Meta keyboard (Meta Quest Browser 26.1+). With hand tracking, pinch index = trigger and pinch middle finger = grip. On desktop your right arm points wherever the mouse cursor points.

🙂 Face, eyes & hands

Your avatar's mouth moves as you talk (microphone lip-sync, with wide smiles on "ee"/"s" sounds and rounded lips on "oo"), it blinks, its eyes follow the wheel or fairy, and the brows and smile react to the emoji you send. If your headset exposes WebXR face tracking (e.g. Quest Pro) the real expression data takes over. Fingers curl with your trigger, grip and thumb. Turn the mic lip-sync off in ⚙️. Sound effects can be switched off and on there too.

✊ Grab, levitate & effects

PC: click an object within arm's reach to hold it (move the mouse to swing, click to drop); click a sofa/chair/beanbag to sit (W/A/S/D to stand); Shift+drag still moves furniture. VR grab: put your hand inside an object and squeeze the grip — it follows your hand like any VR game; swing it, or let go mid-swing to throw it. Levitate: point at an object and hold the grip ~½ second — a beam appears and the object floats at the end of it, following your wrist; release to drop. Spark can now add effects and abilities (trails, flames, glow, rainbow, zap-on-swing, held lights, snow, rain, confetti, fireflies) and build holdable swords and wands — just ask.

🎛️ Move & rotate objects

Mouse/touch: click an object Spark made to select it (cyan ring). Drag it to move it around (its rotation is kept). Drag the cyan ring, or Shift+drag, to rotate it; the scroll wheel or [ ] keys also spin it, Alt+drag tilts, Esc or a click on empty space drops it. Delete: while you drag or hold something, a red ✕ appears (top of the screen for mouse/touch, floating in the room when you hold or levitate it) — drop the item on it to remove it. Effects: items with an ability or light (glow, flame, spin, hover, zap, lamps…) switch off/on when you click them (VR: quick tap of the grip while pointing); on PC, press-and-hold to pick one up instead. VR: point at an object, hold the grip (no trigger) and sweep the controller sideways to spin it. Everything Spark makes is procedurally generated.

Walk around the room and check yourself out in the mirror. WASD / left stick moves, drag looks around.

Settings — Meta / Muse APIs

One Meta API key is used for both Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage), never in this file. With a key: speech goes to Muse Voice Transcribe, the text goes to Muse Spark. Without a key, voice falls back to the headset's built-in speech recognition (the same engine as the keyboard's mic button, started automatically with no keyboard popping up) and Spark falls back to a local translator that turns your words into the best-matching emoji in word order, with redundant symbols pruned.

Clear placed objects removes everything Spark placed (and its mood lighting) from this browser's memory. Spark receives one message: for translation, a short instruction to translate what you said into an emoji sequence of 12 or less; for Ask Spark (trigger-hold, wheel closed), an instruction to actually answer your question helpfully as a separate assistant, using the scene image for context. Lewd or hateful content is not translated.
