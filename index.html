Emoji Room — Press the index trigger on either controller (desktop: press L) to open the emoji wheel — it opens in that hand and stays open; press again to close it. The golden ball rests in the centre. Push the thumbstick of the hand holding the wheel toward an emoji on the rim until the ball turns green, then let go to pick it. Or point your other controller at the ball, hold its trigger (desktop: mouse button) and drag it there, then release. Let go anywhere else — or slide off first — and it returns to the centre. The other controller's thumbstick forward/back still walks you around. The other thumbstick left/right (desktop: Q/E or arrows) flips wheel pages. Closing the wheel sends your picks above your head — it stays up for a few seconds, and you can see it in the mirror.
Voice: hold index + middle-finger (grip) trigger together on one controller and speak (desktop: hold V). Letting go sends; wave your free hand through the transcript box (desktop: Esc) to cancel it.
Right thumbstick left/right turns you (snap turn, while the wheel is closed).
Search: with the wheel open, point your other controller at the search box under the page name and pull its trigger (desktop: click it and just type, Enter to search) — the Meta keyboard opens; results appear on wheel page 2. Settings in VR: point at ⚙️ at the top of your view, pull the trigger, then tap ✏️ to type your API key with the Meta keyboard (Meta Quest Browser 26.1+). Space jumps (desktop).
Walk around the room and check yourself out in the mirror. WASD / left stick moves, drag looks around.

Settings — Meta / Muse APIs

One Meta API key is used for both Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage), never in this file. Voice needs the key: speech goes to Muse Voice Transcribe, the text goes to Muse Spark. Without a key, voice is off.

Spark receives one message: a short instruction to translate what you said into an emoji sequence of 12 or less that captures the full statement in order, without adding anything, plus your words. Lewd or hateful content is not translated.
