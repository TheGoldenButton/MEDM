Emoji Room — Press the index trigger on either controller (desktop: press L) to open the emoji wheel — it opens in that hand and stays open; press again to close it. The golden ball rests in the centre. Point your other controller at the golden ball, hold its trigger (desktop: mouse button) and drag it toward an emoji on the rim until the ball turns green, then let go to pick it. Let go anywhere else — or slide off first — and it returns to the centre. The left thumbstick always walks you around (it does not move the ball), even while the wheel is open. The right thumbstick left/right (desktop: Q/E or arrows) flips wheel pages. Closing the wheel sends your picks above your head — it stays up for a few seconds, and you can see it in the mirror.
Voice: hold index + middle-finger (grip) trigger together on one controller and speak (desktop: hold V) to translate into emoji. Letting go sends; wave your free hand through the transcript box (desktop: Esc) to cancel it.
Translate vs Ask: wheel open + hold the wheel hand's trigger (desktop: hold L), or index + grip together with the wheel closed, = translate into emoji. Wheel closed + hold the index trigger alone = Ask Spark (a direct question, not a translation; in AR it is sent the headset-camera view).
Ask Spark 🧚: with the wheel closed, hold the index trigger on its own (desktop: hold L) — keep holding, speak your question, then let go. A quick tap still opens the wheel. A ball of light forms in front of you while you speak; when Spark has an answer, the fairy emerges from it and hovers a little way back so she stays in view, answering in emoji (it does not translate — it answers, using what you are looking at).
AR Mode 📷: tap AR Mode at the top (or the 📷 button in-headset) to switch to headset-camera passthrough — the VR room disappears, the real world is used as the scene, and Ask Spark / voice use that real-world view to infer what you mean.
Right thumbstick left/right turns you (snap turn, while the wheel is closed).
Search: with the wheel open, point your other controller at the search box under the page name and pull its trigger (desktop: click it and just type, Enter to search) — the Meta keyboard opens; results appear on wheel page 2. Settings in VR: point at ⚙️ at the top of your view, pull the trigger, then tap ✏️ to type your API key with the Meta keyboard (Meta Quest Browser 26.1+). A (right controller) / Space (desktop) jumps. B / Y closes the wheel without sending. With hand tracking, pinch index = trigger, pinch middle finger = grip. On desktop your right arm points wherever the mouse cursor points.
Walk around the room and check yourself out in the mirror. WASD / left stick moves, drag looks around.

Settings — Meta / Muse APIs

One Meta API key is used for both Muse Voice Transcribe and Muse Spark. It is stored only in this browser (localStorage), never in this file. Voice needs the key: speech goes to Muse Voice Transcribe, the text goes to Muse Spark. Without a key, voice is off.

Spark receives one message: for translation, a short instruction to translate what you said into an emoji sequence of 12 or less; for Ask Spark (trigger-hold, wheel closed), an instruction to actually answer your question helpfully as a separate assistant, using the scene image for context. Lewd or hateful content is not translated.
